A Bit About Us

Who Are We?

We are FTC team Bamboozled #36452, a passionate FTC robotics team dedicated to innovation, engineering, our community. Our team is made up of 15 students in grades 8-11 based in Bothell, Washington who bring diverse talents to our team, whether it be presentational skills or excellence in engineering. Our end goal here is to learn together about robotics but at the same time advocate for pandas, an endangered species that need to be heard more in this world.

What Is FTC?

First Tech Challenge (Also known as FTC) is a FIRST Robotics program along with First Lego League (FLL) and First Robotics Challenge (FRC). It is an international robotics program for students in grades 7-12. Teams build, design, and program robots and compete in an alliancebased team robot game (differs every season). When a competition happens, we are ready to run our robot and explain our journey (via a presentation) to the judges.

Robotics and Engineering

One aspect of the First Tech Challenge program is robotics and engineering. Every year, when the season is revealed (end of september), we begin build our robot in order for it to complete the tasks provided and code the robot, which is a big part of the program. Besides robotics and engineering, we have to prepare a presentation explaining our journey this season with an engineering notebook as well. What We Do

As an FTC team, we want to make sure we are doing everything right. In order for that to happen, we make plans post season and before as well. We practice building a robot to learn what goes on behind the scenes, and to learn abut coding, Not only that, sponsorships as well is a big part of our program. Every week, we meet and plan specific tasks to be done, and we complete them to prepare ourselves for the upcoming season Biobuzz.

Why Partner with Us?

Supporting our FTC team is an opportunity to invest directly in students developing the technical and professional skills needed to become the next generation of engineers, programmers, innovators, and leaders. Our team combines competitive robotics with STEM outreach and community engagement. Through sponsorship and mentorship, our partners help us access the equipment, materials, competition opportunities, and educational experiences necessary to continue growing our program.
