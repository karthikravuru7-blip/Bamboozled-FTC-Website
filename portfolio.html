The Bamboozled Philosophy

Do the Work Ourselves

Our team has a goal to actually put in effort. We don't take lazy solutions, we take the BEST solution and modify it to our liking. Instead of using Claude and whatnot to generate our code, we write it ourselves. This way, we can LEARN from our mistakes and better ourselves!

Be Joyful

The best way to be good at what you do is to enjoy what you do. After all, one passionate worker is better than five sad workers in just about anything you can think of. We find ways to enjoy our occupation, so that we can do everything as effectively and efficiently as possible.

Four Teams in One

Coding

Any good robot needs some form of programming. Our wonderful coding team works on figuring out what the bot is meant to do, and making sure it does its job almost perfectly. They're also responsible for this website!

Building

A bot can't be made from just code and a bunch of unattached parts. It has to be assembled. As a result, we have created a group of 4 builders, who will construct the robot and customize its appearance to our liking. After all, what good is everything else if it's not put together?

CAD

Not all parts can easily be found on Amazon or Newegg. However, that's what our CAD team is here for. They design parts for the robot that can't be found anywhere else. They're also good for giving us a general blueprint of what the bot looks like, so that the building team has at least some reference as to what they have to build.

Outreach

We can build robots all day, but what good is it if no one knows of our team's existence to support us? The outreach team cooperates with local businesses to benefit both the team and said local business, while also spreading word of our existence. They also run our social media pages, so just know that they're responsible for our high quality content!
